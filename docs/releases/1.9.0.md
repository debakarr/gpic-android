## Smoother transitions

- Zoom, spin and slide transitions no longer stop dead at the cut: the move carries through it with motion blur, and never shows black edges or a dark dip.
- **Dissolve** (new) is a real crossfade, and **Blur** and **Slide** now show both clips at once.
- New **Whip left/right** and **Glitch** transitions.
- Camera sound now fades briefly at each cut, and longer under fades and dissolves.

## Photos

- Picking several photos makes a slideshow: each photo gets its own slow zoom or pan, and they dissolve into each other.
- Up to 200 photos and clips in one edit (it was 80, and the rest were left out without a word).
- The editor's preview loads photos at preview size, which uses far less memory.

## Smart edit, templates and Claude

- Smart edit uses its style's transition every few cuts instead of on every cut (Vlog used to flash white every 5 s), adds whips that follow the camera's pans, and keeps photos moving in every style.
- New 2 min length in Smart edit.
- Templates use the new transitions; slideshow templates dissolve between photos.
- Photos in Claude's edits get a slow move when Claude doesn't choose one.

## Long videos

- Clips longer than 2 minutes are measured properly: shaky and steady shots are told apart across the whole clip instead of being guessed from frames seconds apart.
- A clip is analysed once and remembered, not again every time GPic starts.
- Claude is told about shots from the whole clip, not just its first minutes.
- The timeline draws only what's on screen, so long clips don't slow it down.
