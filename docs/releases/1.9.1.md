## Fixes

- **Clear camera downloads.** Files downloaded from a DJI camera over WiFi (kept on the phone so a cancelled or failed upload can retry without the camera) are now listed in the Camera tab with their size, with a **Clear** button that deletes them. They used to stay on the phone indefinitely, and Android's "Clear cache" doesn't remove them because they live in the app's external files folder (`Android/data/com.gpic.android/files/camera-staging`).
- Clearing deletes both complete copies and partial downloads; the files themselves stay on the camera, and uploading them again downloads them again. Nothing is cleared while an upload is running.
